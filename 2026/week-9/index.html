02 / THE SIGNATURE GAME

Vi drew the wrong week.

The fourth-best lineup ran straight into the best score of the season.

Full story 1 min read ＋

ivan1265 191.54 · Voltron Fc 131.38

Ivan hit 146.58 to end Rob’s winning streak in Week 8. He added 44.96 on top of that against Vi and beat his own Week 4 league high of 186.62. Walker led with 36.30, Nacua followed with 35.00 and Burrow put up 29.54. Javonte Williams, acquired from Gianni in Week 2, added 22.40.

That’s three straight wins after Ivan lost back-to-back games to Farhan and Cho. The Burrow-Walker-Nacua haul supplies the headline, and Williams is the reason the top end looks so unfair. He was part of a separate trade. Four different acquired starters cleared 22 points in the same week.

Vi had a lineup that would normally pay. Bucky Irving led it with 28.50. Bijan Robinson scored 26.70 and Patrick Mahomes added 16.48, giving Vi 43.18 from the two players he acquired from Gianni in Week 8. In Week 8 he beat Farhan with 107.18. He improved by 24.20 this week and moved back below .500.

Vi gets Raif next. His 4–5 record leaves a one-game gap to the cut. The bigger problem comes later. He plays Rob in Week 11, Gianni in Week 13 and Kamar in Week 14. A win over Raif is the chance to get to .500 before those games.

Ivan’s Week 10 replacement problem

Nacua finished with 150 receiving yards and a touchdown against Washington. He injured his knee and toe on the Rams’ final offensive snap. Sean McVay said Monday that injured reserve was likely, and NFL Network reported the placement. The Week 10 model gives Nacua’s roster spot to Reggie Virgil alongside Amon-Ra St. Brown. The league’s best score just lost one of its biggest contributors.

05 / USED POINTS ONLY

The trade ledger got uglier.

The Raif deal is up to a 300.92-point gap. Gianni gets a return from his newest deal.

Full story 2 min read ＋

The Week 3 blockbuster

Through Week 9, Ivan has used 564.62 starter points from the original assets he got from Raif. Raif has used 251.50 from his original assets and another 12.20 from Jacory Croskey-Merritt and Wan’Dale Robinson in the traced chain. That’s 564.62 against 263.70. Last week the gap was 230.64. This week’s 110.24 from Ivan’s original package and 39.96 from Raif’s pushed it to 300.92.

Raif has useful starters in Allen, Jefferson and Kelce, but he sold the better production. The ledger counts actual starts and keeps subsequent returns visible. It cannot assign wins or tell either manager what a lineup without the deal would have scored.

Gianni and Vi made a second deal

Mahomes and Robinson have supplied Vi 50.78 across Weeks 8–9. Judkins and Olave have supplied Gianni 29.20. Gianni’s side of the newer deal paid sooner. He acquired Stevenson on Nov 7 for Breece Hall and Tre Tucker, then started Stevenson for 14.80 against Farhan. Hall’s 11.70 sat on Vi’s bench. Vi dropped Tucker before Tucker’s Week 9 kickoff. That’s 14.80 used for Gianni and zero used for Vi so far from the new deal.

Gianni beat Farhan. Watson still won the trade.

Watson’s 30.10 pushes his used return for Farhan to 144.20 since Week 2. Rome Odunze and Khalil Shakir produced 6.80 for Gianni from the original assets. Even including 38.30 from T.J. Hockenson, the end of the Odunze-to-Higbee-to-Hockenson waiver path, Gianni’s broadened return is 45.10. That’s a 99.10-point gap. Stevenson’s 14.80 belongs to a separate deal and helped beat Farhan this week.

Higgins keeps paying Ibrahim

Ibrahim used 20.20 from Higgins to beat Cho. Through Week 9 the original assets he received in the Week 3 deal with Ivan have supplied 151.20 used points, against 32.90 for Ivan’s incoming assets. Ivan can lead the league and still be on the wrong side of this one.
