Playoff scenario

Every team keeps its actual record, points for and points against through Week 9. For each remaining fixture the model finds the highest projected legal lineup from the current active roster, with reserve, taxi and NFL bye players excluded. It uses Sleeper’s RotoWire PPR as a common scoring proxy for First Take’s scoring. Empty slots score zero in the projection.

The main run draws 200,000 seasons (seed 202603). It adds a season-long team offset with SD 7.6 and weekly score noise with SD 25, rounds scores to hundredths and floors them at zero. Seeds are ordered by record, points for and points against. Each run allocates six playoff places and two byes. Four 100,000-season checks use weekly SD 20 or 35 and separate common projection scales of 90% and 110%. All runs exclude Nacua. Scenario ranges describe the changes in score variance and projection scale. Future roster moves, manager lineup errors and new injuries are outside the model.

The model excludes Nacua in Weeks 10–14, covering the Rams’ next four games and their Week 11 bye. The Rams’ Nov 9 injury update documents the knee sprains and turf toe. NFL Network reported his IR placement. The forward scenario applies that injury news separately from the Week 9 score ledger. Jake Ferguson (Weeks 10 and 11), Mike Evans (Week 10) and Josh Jacobs (Week 10) lack projections and show Out in the provider snapshot, so those player-weeks are excluded. Kendre Miller lacks a listed NFL team and is excluded. These are explicit modeling assumptions about the input snapshot.
