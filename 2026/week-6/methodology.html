Qualification model

Main run: 200,000 seasons, seed 202603. Six observed weekly scores, actual Weeks 7-14 schedule, six playoff places and two byes. Team strength is drawn from a Normal posterior with league scoring mean as prior center, prior SD 15 and weekly SD 25. Posterior variance=1/(1/15²+6/25²); posterior mean weight=6×15²/(25²+6×15²)=0.683544. Conditional on each strength draw, future weekly totals are independent Normal draws with SD 25, rounded to hundredths and floored at zero. Simulated ties count half a win. Sorting: wins, points for, points against. Exactly six playoff places, two byes and 70 season wins are conserved in every simulation.

Four sensitivity runs use 100,000 seasons each and prior/weekly SD pairs (10,25), (20,25), (10,35), (20,35), seed 202603. Displayed spread is the minimum and maximum across all five models, not a confidence interval. Injury, player, NFL-bye, waiver and trade forecasts are omitted. These are scoring-model estimates, not calibrated player-based probabilities or mathematical clinch proofs.
