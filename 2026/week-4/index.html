02 / AROUND THE LEAGUE

Vi wanted the league. Gianni took the win.

Gianni escaped by 4.94. Rob’s stars delivered, Farhan won comfortably, and Ibrahim is still chasing win number one.

Full story4 min read ＋

Pirate Flag 129.70, Voltron FC 124.76: Wilson paid off immediately

Gianni added Emanuel Wilson before Week 4 and put him straight into the lineup for 27.00. Wilson and Bijan Robinson combined for 54.70, with another 20.60 from Brock Bowers to help cover Parker Washington's 2.00. The 4.94-point win was Gianni's third straight after losing in Week 1.

His recent message to the league was laugh now, cry later. At 3–1, he gets another week to enjoy that one.

Vi has been declaring that the league is his, which makes falling to 2–2 a little awkward. Quinshon Judkins contributed 21.60 and Chris Olave 19.60, but Dalton Schultz and DJ Moore combined for 5.60. Deebo Samuel scored 18.00 on the bench. Replacing Moore's 2.70 in the FLEX would have produced 140.06 and a win.

That swap is easy to make after seeing the scores. Whether it was the right call before kickoff is a separate argument.

HOW HE GOT HERE

Vi acquired Deebo from Ivan for Travis Kelce on September 14. Deebo's 18.00 was available on Vi's Week 4 bench. The deal gave him an option; hindsight tells us what that option would have scored.

Completed trade sources

Gianni is also third in four-week all-play at 25–11, behind Ameed and Ivan. The narrow win deserves some luck talk; his season has been better than this one margin suggests.

AsianPersuasion 157.76, Glen Burnie Goons 147.98: Kamar had just added Doubs

Rob got 78.00 from CeeDee Lamb and Kyren Williams, then 17.40 from Devaughn Vele and 17.00 from George Kittle. He won by 9.78 to reach 2–2. He's already exchanging Week 7 challenges with Farhan, and the schedule confirms they'll get their game.

Kamar scored 147.98, fifth among ten teams this week, and still fell to 1–3. Zay Flowers supplied 25.80 and Sam LaPorta 22.40. The painful part: Kamar had added Romeo Doubs before Week 4, then left his 23.80 on the bench. In hindsight, using Doubs instead of Matthew Golden's 11.70 would have yielded 160.08.

The Goons have more season points than Asian, which gives Kamar something to complain about. Both teams are 17–19 in four-week all-play comparisons, though. The larger point total hasn't translated into beating more of the field each week.

Cold Rome 149.78, COKEHEADS 102.88: Farhan gets Ivan next

Farhan returned to the win column by 46.90, Week 4's largest margin. Nico Collins led with 30.80, followed by Malik Nabers at 23.20, Jonathan Taylor at 22.70 and Chase Brown at 19.10. Those four alone produced 96.80.

HOW HE GOT HERE

Farhan acquired Nico from Ivan for David Montgomery on September 16. Nico led Rome's Week 4 scoring. Now Farhan faces the manager who sent him that receiver.

Completed trade sources

Cho got 16.50 from Michael Wilson and 15.90 from Derrick Henry, but Ja'Marr Chase and Emeka Egbuka combined for 9.00. COKEHEADS finished with the week's second-lowest total and a third straight defeat.

Rome is now 3–1 with 580.92 season points; Cho is 1–3 with 477.52. Farhan and Rob have a Week 7 argument underway, but unbeaten Ivan gets the next shot at Rome. Cho draws Gianni's Pirate Flag, which is coming off three straight wins.

Starting Denzel Boston instead of Emeka Egbuka and Isaiah Likely instead of Josh Downs would have lifted Cho to 121.48, still 28.30 short. Both swaps fit the lineup rules. Even the hindsight repair leaves Farhan with a comfortable win; Ivan is a tougher assignment next week.

GWIDDYIN 111.42, imalik 94.52: Raif gets his first win

Raif got his first win with the week's lowest winning score. Chuba Hubbard and Alvin Kamara combined for 48.70, and Josh Allen added 19.52. That covered Ladd McConkey's recorded zero and Travis Kelce's 3.50. The Chargers confirmed McConkey was active before kickoff; their Monday update says he played 24 snaps before being ruled out with a foot injury. His zero came in a game he was available to start.

Ibrahim got 26.70 from Tee Higgins and 16.30 from James Cook, while Rashee Rice recorded zero and Dalton Kincaid 1.70. The Chiefs' October 5 recap confirms Rice left the game early with a hamstring injury. The 16.90-point loss left Ibrahim 0–4. Rice's exit cut his game short; it cannot tell us what a healthy Rice would have scored.

He had added Will Reichard before Week 4, then left the kicker's 19.00 on the bench. Two hindsight swaps would have changed the result: Tucker Kraft for Kincaid adds 14.80 and still leaves Ibrahim short; Reichard for Evan McPherson adds another 14.00, taking him to 123.32.

It's a winning lineup now that all the scores are known. Ibrahim still has to defend those choices as decisions he would have made before kickoff.

Raif now gets Ameed. His winning 111.42 was below all four Glock scores so far; Ameed’s lowest was 145.18. Raif will probably need more to make this a streak, though neither team’s past score decides Week 5.

Injury reporting: Chargers inactives, Oct 4; Chargers update, Oct 5; Chiefs recap, Oct 5.

HOW HE GOT HERE
