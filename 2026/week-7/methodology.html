Qualification model

Main run: 200,000 seasons, seed 202603. Seven observed weekly scores, actual Weeks 8-14 schedule, six playoff places and two byes. Team strength is drawn from a Normal posterior centered on the league scoring mean, prior SD 15 and weekly SD 25. Posterior variance=1/(1/15²+7/25²); mean weight=7×15²/(25²+7×15²)=0.715909. Conditional on each season-strength draw, future scores are independent Normal draws with SD 25, rounded to hundredths and floored at zero. Ties count half a win. Sorting: wins, points for, points against. Every simulation conserves six playoff places, two byes and 70 season wins.

Four sensitivity runs use 100,000 seasons each and prior/weekly SD pairs (10,25), (20,25), (10,35), (20,35), seed 202603. Spread is the minimum and maximum across all five runs, not a confidence interval. Injuries, player projections, NFL byes, waiver and trade forecasts are omitted. These estimates are not calibrated player-based probabilities or mathematical clinch proofs.
