01 / THE FREE WIN

Raif had already lost his season. He still got to ruin somebody else’s Sunday.

Full story 1 min read ＋

Raif 146.16 · Gianni 123.06

At 1:00 a.m. Friday, Gianni declared a free win over a guy who’d started Xavier Hutchinson. Fair enough about Hutchinson. He gave Raif 3.30. Unfortunately for Gianni, the other nine slots gave Raif 142.86. That alone would have beaten him.

Chuba Hubbard hung 26.00. Travis Kelce added 25.40, Ladd McConkey 20.70, and Chicago’s defense 17.00. Raif finished 0.14 behind Ivan for third on the week. His second win took 23.10 points and a seed off Gianni.

Emmett Johnson gave Gianni 24.30, and Tyler Bass kicked in 17.00. Michael Mayer, Garrett Wilson and Caleb Douglas totaled 13.30. Gianni spent Friday auditing another man’s FLEX and got 1.40 from his own tight end. Now he’s 6–5, in fifth, with Rob next. By Monday night he had a new explanation. It was already playing for Ivan.

02 / THE TRADE SLIP

Gianni spent Saturday trading his Monday excuse.

Croskey-Merritt gave Ivan 15.70. Gianni still loses even if you give the points back.

Full story 1 min read ＋

On Monday Gianni wrote, “if i started jcm raif would be fried”. At 1:18 a.m. Saturday he’d already shipped Jacory Croskey-Merritt to Ivan for Jordan Love. Gianni can complain about the trade. He can’t complain about leaving a man on a bench he no longer owned.

Give him the deal back and put the 15.70 in Caleb Douglas’s 5.00 FLEX slot. That’s 133.76. Raif still wins by 12.40. The trade could still pay Gianni back once Love plays for him. Its only submitted Week 11 return was Ivan’s 15.70.

Ivan 146.30 · Kamar 110.48

Croskey-Merritt returned to Ivan after stops with Raif and Gianni. Javonte Williams, the other back Ivan got from Gianni in Week 2, led this win with 24.10. Antonio Williams added 21.20. Ivan went from the league’s worst score to its third best without starting Puka Nacua.

Kamar got 46.30 from McCaffrey against Farhan and lost. This time he got 14.20 and lost again. Jameson Williams’s 19.30 led his team, while Keon Coleman and Kyle Monangai combined for 5.50. The Goons still have sixth. If Kamar wants to keep it, the guy who just made Gianni eat his words is standing right there.

His actual league-winner receipt, Jaylen Warren, put up 4.90 and sits at 154.80 across ten starts. Gibbs has 72.00 in the last two alone. If this team takes the trophy, let’s see whose picture ends up on the front.

The Week 10 receiver swap is already staring Gianni in the face before Week 12.

Vi has a better shot than the man in his chair.

The standings give Kamar sixth. The remaining schedule gives Vi a way through him.

Full story 1 min read ＋

Ameed and Ivan are in. Each has eight wins, and of the teams currently fifth or lower, only Gianni and one of Kamar or Vi can get to eight. Kamar and Vi play each other in Week 14. There aren’t enough teams left that can catch either 8–3 club, even giving away every PF tie. The byes still have to be earned.

Rob, Gianni, Kamar and Vi compete for the bottom half of the bracket with Farhan still catchable behind the leaders. Vi’s final three opponents are Cho, Gianni and Kamar. He gets to punch holes in his rivals’ records himself. The model gives him 78.80% to reach the field. It gives Kamar 63.96%. Kamar’s 35.30 PF cushion is real, and so is the date on that Week 14 appointment.

Cho sits at 9.83% in this scenario. Ibrahim remains alive at 3–8 and made it in two of 20,000 runs. Raif is mathematically out. Feel free to keep betting your reputation on beating him.

08 / WEEK 12

Pick the game you’d least like to lose.

The people who spent Week 11 talking now have to play the people who scored.

Full story 1 min read ＋

Ameed vs Farhan

Ameed can go two games clear with two left. Farhan can clinch and pull level at eight wins. Dak covered for nine other starters against Ibrahim. Against Ameed, that same 127.30 would’ve been 39.30 short. Ask the other nine what they’re bringing.

Gianni vs Rob

Win and Rob reaches eight and clinches. Lose and both are 7–5. Gianni enters with 51.12 more PF. There’s your reply to anyone who thinks the Pickens trade gets the last word before kickoff.

Cho vs Vi · Raif vs Kamar · Ivan vs Ibrahim

Cho can pull even with Vi at 5–7 and start an actual fight over the cut. Vi can go to 6–6 and leave Cho needing both remaining wins to finish 6–8, plus help to qualify. Kamar has to do what Gianni just spent four days promising. Ibrahim needs to beat the second seed to keep a path to six wins. Ivan’s next two opponents after that are Ameed and Farhan. He has plenty of reason to take the win now.

Editorial lean: Vi. Low confidence. The headline lives or dies on what happens after that: Vi has Gianni and Kamar in consecutive weeks. And if someone calls Raif a free win again, get it in writing.
