Roster- and bye-aware qualification scenario

The current actual record, PF and PA remain fixed through Week 8. For each future week, Sleeper-supplied RotoWire PPR projections are maximized across the legal QB/RB/RB/WR/WR/TE/FLEX/FLEX/K/DEF lineup from the current active roster. Each player can fill only one slot. NFL teams missing from that week’s full ESPN schedule are on bye and their players are unavailable; eligible bench replacements compete jointly for the remaining slots. Fantasy reserve and taxi players are excluded and held excluded through Week 14. Empty slots receive zero projected points.

Main run: 200,000 seasons, seed 202603. Each team receives a season-long Normal offset with SD 7.6. Future weekly scores are independent Normal draws around that week’s projected legal lineup total plus its team offset, SD 25, rounded to hundredths and floored at zero. These variation assumptions are not fitted provider forecast errors. Existing record/PF are fixed; historical bye effects are not subtracted from an old team mean. Sort record, higher PF, then higher PA. Each season conserves six playoff places, two playoff byes and 70 regular-season wins.

Four sensitivity runs use 100,000 seasons each, seed 202603: weekly SD 20 or 35 at unchanged projections; or every projection scaled to 90% or 110% at SD 25. The displayed minimum/maximum across five runs is assumption sensitivity, not a confidence interval. The prior score-only results are retained locally as an explicit comparison; this new scenario changes the entire future roster projection basis as well as accounting for NFL byes.
