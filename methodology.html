## Model

200,000 seasons; random seed 202603. Observations are each team's actual starting-lineup scores in Weeks 1-4. Prior team mean: league average 133.908; prior strength SD 15 points. Weekly residual SD 25 points (deliberately above the observed pooled SD 19.052). Normal-normal posterior mean weights four-week average by 59.016%, league average by 40.984%; posterior mean SD 9.603 points.

Draw each team's latent scoring mean once per simulated season. Draw independent weekly Normal scores around that mean with SD 25; floor at zero and round to 0.01. Use the actual retrieved Week 5-14 pairings. A weekly tie adds half a standings win. Rank by win equivalents, higher PF, higher PA. Count seeds 1-6 as playoffs, 1-2 as bye. No championship odds are implied.



Sensitivity: four additional 100,000-season runs cross prior SD {10,20} with weekly SD {25,35}; displayed spread spans these and the main model. These are assumption ranges, not confidence intervals. The main Monte Carlo sampling error is at most about 0.22 percentage points for a 95% interval; model uncertainty is much larger. Rounded main playoff odds sum to 600%; the unrounded probabilities sum to exactly 600%.



Limitations: only four games of evidence; no player-level forecasts, injuries, NFL bye adjustments, trade/waiver effects, opponent correlations, future scoring changes or commissioner overrides. Current strength is uncertain, not known. Current team names are used as labels; do not infer that they were identical in earlier weeks. League chat was not refreshed, so no chat quotes, current rivalries or personal allegations are included.
