Rolling-waiver forecast

20,000 trials, seed 20261124, captured November 24 at 10:59 a.m. ET. Weeks 12–14 use actual fantasy pairings, six playoff places and two byes. Sleeper-served projected statistics were scored with league rules. No Week 12–14 Subvertadown ranking inputs were imported. There were 36 rostered player-weeks without usable projected means.

Claims fill known lineup holes in rolling priority and persist into future weeks. Legal lineups re-optimize after simulated availability. Questionable plays in half the trials and doubtful in a tenth. Bye and hard-unavailable players do not play. Current designations are not projected into future weeks. Player scores use independent position-level variance and projected means. Trades, manager preferences, timing of actual claims and correlated scoring are outside the model.
