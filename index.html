01 / FRONT PAGE

How much fucking more, Kamar?

Christian McCaffrey gave him 46.30. Kamar gave him three seconds of praise. Farhan won by 15.10.

Full story 1 min read ＋

At 7:23:46 Sunday night, Kamar looked at what Christian McCaffrey had done for him and wrote “46 points is ode”. At 7:23:49 he added “but i want more”. Three seconds. Christian had 223 yards from scrimmage and three touchdowns. What does a running back have to do to get a full minute out of this ungrateful motherfucker?

Eleven seconds later came the purpose of all that work: “i need far to understand there’s levels”. Farhan finished with the third-highest score in First Take. Kamar finished seventh. Count down four levels from Farhan and there’s Kamar.

McCaffrey caught all four of his targets for 161 yards and two touchdowns, and added 62 yards and another score on the ground. San Francisco beat Dallas 46–40. Kamar got 46.30 fantasy points on the afternoon the entire 49ers team put 46 real points on the scoreboard. Dak Prescott, on the losing sideline, threw five touchdown passes. Farhan had picked him up Thursday.

By 9:26 Sunday, Kamar had revised the lesson: “yea my team is cooked i celebrated too early”. Fourteen seconds later, Vi wrote “Welcome back”. That was the whole reply. Kamar had introduced the levels, located his and returned to the group in two hours and three minutes.

Farhan won 149.32–134.22 and stopped a three-game skid. He also opened his own mouth before Monday’s game was finished, which is a ridiculous thing to do while lecturing another man about celebrating early. His waiver claim deserves credit. His lecture deserves to be saved.

02 / THE SIGNATURE GAME

Gianni put the quarterback on waivers.

Wednesday: dropped. Thursday: claimed. Sunday: five touchdown passes for Farhan.

Full story 3 min read ＋

Cold Rome 149.32 · Glen Burnie Goons 134.22

A week ago Gianni beat Farhan 130.20–123.06. On Wednesday he dropped Prescott. Farhan’s waiver claim went through Thursday, and on Sunday Dak gave Cold Rome 35.42. Gianni then contributed “kamar bouta finish farhan off” to the live commentary. From anybody else, that’s a prediction. From the guy who put Dak on waivers, it reads like a man standing over a grease fire explaining how dinner’s going to be ready any minute.

Farhan still had to claim Prescott and start him. That choice paid. Aaron Rodgers gave Kamar two touchdown passes and 15.62 fantasy points; Dak beat him by 19.80. Minnesota’s defense added 21 for Farhan against Seattle’s 10 for Kamar. Across those two slots, Cold Rome gained 30.80. Across the other eight combined, the Goons led 108.60–92.90. Kamar spent the evening asking the wrong employee for overtime.

Chase Brown caught eight passes and took two in for scores. Nico Collins drew 16 targets, caught nine and scored. They supplied 52.70 together, giving Farhan enough production around his quarterback to survive McCaffrey. That’s two players against one, so spare us the efficiency lecture. They still had to do the fucking work.

Farhan can ease off the genius act, too. Stefon Diggs had six catches, 83 yards, a touchdown and 20.30 on his bench while Christian Watson scored 6.70 and Malik Nabers 7.40 in his lineup. The score-only ceiling from Farhan’s recorded pool is 166.92. Kamar’s is exactly his submitted 134.22. Even after rearranging every observed point into position slots, the Goons finish 15.10 behind Farhan’s actual ten.

That hindsight check ignores kickoff locks, acquisition timing and availability. It establishes neither a sensible pregame Diggs choice nor a legally available perfect lineup. It does establish why blaming an imaginary stash of Kamar bench points would be bullshit.

The warning came early, too

Kamar got 14.80 from Flowers and Vidal on Monday. Flowers lost a fumble at 11:09 p.m.; 85 seconds later Kamar sent “zay”. Lowercase prayer? A Baltimore fan watching the Chargers recover? The performance review department trying to fit somebody else under the bus? He gave us three letters during the game. Give Flowers the two fumble points back and Farhan still wins by 13.10.

Farhan had already sent “good game kamar” at 10:05, followed by “you need a better game plan” and “and so do i”. At 10:07 came “don’t celebrate too early against me”. There were still more than eleven minutes left in the third quarter. This motherfucker was celebrating early while giving Kamar instructions on when to celebrate. Keep those messages together for the week it goes wrong.

The season-series boast has a narrower, better foundation. On November 9 Farhan wrote “kamar i own you”. Kamar answered “u got one dub on me i wouldn’t say own”. Farhan’s next word was “true”. He made the second win a condition of his next ownership claim, and Kamar answered “nah😂” and “but let us see”.

Now they have played twice in the 2026 First Take season. Farhan won 171.86–152.80 in Week 1 and by 15.10 here. That earns a 2–0 brag, with no all-time mythology attached. Kamar hung 152.80 on him once and got 46.30 from a single back the next time. Both would sound like wins if you wandered off halfway through the box score. Keep “but let us see”, Kamar. Retire “one dub”.

Four Sundays, six seats.

A frozen-roster scenario before future moves. Raif’s exit is already arithmetic.

Full story 2 min read ＋

Vi is outside the cut today and better positioned than three teams above him in this frozen-roster scenario. He has Rob next, then Cho, Gianni and Kamar. His 81.9% playoff estimate beats Kamar’s 70.6% and Rob’s 46.7%. The table rewards the remaining roster and schedule under stated assumptions. It does not award Vi a playoff place for being good at Tuesday.

Rob’s number needs its ugly footnote in the daylight. New England and the Rams are on bye in Week 11. With his current active roster and no future additions allowed, the projection leaves quarterback and a second running-back slot empty. That is a problem to fix, not a forecast that Rob will actually submit two holes. Kamar’s Week 11 scenario also has an empty defense slot. Managers can use waivers; this model cannot. Treat the damage from those gaps accordingly.

Ameed and Ivan remain the strongest bets at 99.9% and 99.6%. Those rounded values establish no clinch. Farhan has 90.4%, but his remaining opponents are Ibrahim, Ameed, Cho and Ivan. Cho’s scoring surge meets a 23.7% route from 4–6. Ibrahim’s 3–7 start to the final month leaves 6.3%. The distinction matters to next week’s football: they are playing Ameed and Farhan, respectively, while Rob and Vi face one another above them. They need results against the top half before anybody else’s loss becomes useful.

Raif’s exit is arithmetic

Raif can win all four remaining games and finish only 5–9. Five teams already have at least six wins. Kamar and Vi each have five and play each other in Week 14, guaranteeing another team ahead of Raif: a winner reaches six; a tie still leaves a better record than 5–9. Under the league’s record-ranked six-team format, Raif is mathematically eliminated. That is a schedule-derived conclusion, not an official Sleeper label and not a deduction from a rounded zero in a simulation. Four games left, and even a perfect finish cannot get his ass through the door.
